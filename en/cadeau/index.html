Frequently asked questions

Can I give the subscription directly?

No, Apple doesn’t allow subscriptions to be given as gifts. An Apple Gift Card does work: the credit pays for the subscription.

What if there’s credit left over?

It stays on the Apple Account and can be used for other App Store purchases.

Does it work outside the Netherlands?

Yes. Apple Gift Cards work the same way in every country where they’re sold; buy one for the country of the parents’ Apple Account.
