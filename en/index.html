Meet Romeo and Giorgio

Romeo is four and his dog Giorgio goes everywhere with him. Together they explain every game, give a hint when something’s tricky and celebrate every star.

How it started

Romeo and Giorgio are real. Romeo is our 4-year-old son and Giorgio is our dog. When Romeo wanted to play on the iPad, we looked for fun games he would actually learn something from. We couldn't find them: most apps were full of ads, flashing buttons and things to buy.

So we started making our own. Together with Romeo we came up with games at the kitchen table: counting apples, reading the clock, guessing which sound you hear. Romeo tried everything and told us honestly what he found boring. Giorgio was always there.

That became 64 games, with Romeo and Giorgio explaining each one. Exactly the app we were looking for.

Frequently asked questions

What age is the app for?

For children aged 4 to 6 (reception and year 1). Your child doesn’t need to read yet: Romeo says everything out loud.

Does it work on iPhone?

For now, only on iPad. That way the games are big enough for little fingers.

Can I use the app for more than one child?

Yes. With Family Sharing, your whole family uses one subscription, on every iPad.

How do I cancel?

Through the App Store: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. You keep everything until the end of the paid period.

Is the app safe?

There are no ads, no chat and no account. We don’t collect any data. Purchases and outside links sit behind a question for parents.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All games and voices are on the iPad, so it works in the car or on holiday too.

Which languages are available?

Dutch, English, German and French. In English, Romeo teaches English sounds and letters, plus first Dutch words. Choose the language in the app, behind the parent question.
