Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

The app

Romeo & Giorgio is a learning app for young children. Children’s privacy comes first for us.

What data do we collect? None. The app does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking services are used.

What stays on the iPad? Progress in the games, the chosen settings and, if a parent enters it, the child’s first name for the game "My name". This data is stored only on the device and goes nowhere else. If you delete the app, it is deleted too.

Photos in "My family". If a parent chooses photos, they stay on the device and are not sent anywhere.

Purchases. Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive any payment details.

Outside links (such as this page) can only be reached after a question for parents.

This website

On this website we use a Meta (Facebook/Instagram) cookie to measure which ads work. This only concerns your visit to the website, never the app. If you prefer not to, you can block Meta cookies in your browser settings or with an ad blocker. If you leave your email address, we only use it to let you know when the app is available. You can unsubscribe at any time.
